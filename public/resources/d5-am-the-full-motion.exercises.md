# Day 5 morning: exercises

FDE School, by Devoteam and OSS Ventures. Participant handout. Teams 1 to 3 work on case pack A (the compounder), teams 4 and 5 on case pack B (the sock manufacturer). Both cases are real engagements, disguised: add nothing that is not in your pack.

## Timing

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 9:15 | Read the pack alone, ten minutes, no talking |
| 9:25 | Diagnostic: problem statement and hypothesis tree, by 9:45 |
| 9:45 | Sizing, with the Verifier checking every number, by 10:30 |
| 10:30 | Validation protocol and ninety-day plan, by 11:00 |
| 11:00 | Readout slides, frozen at 11:15 |
| 11:15 | Rehearsal |
| 11:30 | Steering committee: ten minutes, then five of questions |

Roles today: the Lead chosen by the trainer from those who led least this week.

## Readout template (five slides at most)

| Slide | Title (a full-sentence conclusion) | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The answer | Governing thought: the problem, the size with its condition, the decision you ask for |
| 2 | The diagnostic | Problem statement, the tree with priors, what you tested or would test first |
| 3 | The size | Assumptions table, low, base and high, overlaps removed, run-rate or realized labeled |
| 4 | How the gain will be proven | Baseline, formula, data source, who computes it, sign-off trigger |
| 5 | The ninety days | Phases, gates, kill criteria, roles, Monday's first action |

## Checklist before 11:15

- Every number has a source line and a unit.
- No gain is framed as fewer people.
- The baseline is computed by the client's controller, not by you.
- You can say what would make you stop.
- Your Verifier has recomputed the base case.
