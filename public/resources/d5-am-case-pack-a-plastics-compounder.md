# Day 5 morning: case pack A. The plastics compounder

FDE School, by Devoteam and OSS Ventures. Participant handout for teams 1, 2 and 3. **This case is drawn from a real engagement and disguised.** Names, sites and people are invented; financial figures are scaled by a constant factor and rounded; dates are shifted. If you think you recognize the company, keep it to yourself and do not add anything that is not in this pack.

## The situation

**Corvane Compounds** makes specialty vinyl compounds and extruded hose in four plants. The lead plant, **Site North**, compounds flexible and rigid vinyl on a set of extrusion lines with hundreds of active product references. Two other plants run hose extrusion; the fourth is smaller and mixed.

Planning runs on the ERP's scheduling module and on the judgment of two planners at Site North. Demand is volatile: a growing share is made to order (MTO) in small lots, on top of a stable made-to-stock (MTS) base. Every planner meeting starts with the same complaint: changeovers eat the week, and the plan built on Monday no longer holds on Wednesday.

**The sponsor**, Dana Whitfield, VP Operations for the group, has budget for a pilot at Site North. Her message to you:

> "We know we leave throughput on the table. I need to know where, how much, and what you would do in the next ninety days. If it works at North, I want it at the other three sites. Steering committee at 11:30: the plant manager, our controller and me."

## Your task (9:15 to 11:15)

1. **Diagnostic.** Where does Site North lose throughput, and what do you not know yet? Write the problem statement and a hypothesis tree.
2. **Sizing.** Size the opportunity at Site North with an assumptions table, a low, base and high case, and a second path. Then size the four-site prize, with the conditions.
3. **Validation protocol.** What would Glen Moretti need, in week one, to sign the gain? Be specific about the baseline that does not exist yet.
4. **Ninety-day plan.** Phases, gates, kill criteria, roles, the first thing you do on Monday at Site North.
5. **Readout.** Prepare a ten-minute steering committee readout: five slides at most, answer first.

Use any tool you built this week. AI tools are allowed; every number must be checked by your Verifier.

## Steering committee (11:30)

Ten minutes per team, then five minutes of questions from the trainer playing Dana, Tom and Glen.
